What is the MCPS?

What do we do?

The Maple County Paranormal (MCPS) Society is a small team of explorers and investigators originating in Maple County, Oregon.

Our goal is to keep citizens safe from any inexplicable dangers by reserching, preparing, and informing.

If you've had a strange encounter in your home or suspect that something other-worldy may be going on, give us a call!

We will arrive on-site to investigate and deal with any potential hazards. We work efficiently and effectively with professional equipment and a low cost.

We operates in Maple County and Dirt County, as well as parts of Malheur and Baker County. We are planning on expanding our area of operation soon.

What can you do?

The Maple County Paranormal Society benefits from any information, even if it is just theoretical.

We are available 24/7, and if you have any information on an ongoing case or have recently made a new discovery, don't be scared to let us know!

Volunteers who assist in discovering/completing a mission may be rewarded with a portion of the mission payment.
