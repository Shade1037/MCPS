What is the MCPS?

What do we do?

The Maple County Paranormal Society (MCPS) is an independent investigative group based in Maple County, Oregon. We consist of a small team of local explorers, researchers, and field investigators working to document and respond to unexplained phenomena.

Our primary goal is to help keep residents of Maple County and surrounding regions safe by researching, preparing for, and responding to anomalous or otherwise unexplained events. This includes both historical research and active field investigation when necessary.

If you have experienced something unusual in your home, workplace, or surrounding area, or believe you may have encountered something outside normal explanation, you are encouraged to contact us as soon as possible.

Upon receiving a report, MCPS staff may conduct an on-site investigation depending on severity, accessibility, and current operational priority. We aim to respond efficiently and with appropriate equipment to assess and, where possible, mitigate potential hazards.

The MCPS currently operates primarily within Maple County and Dirt County, with additional field activity in select areas of Malheur County and Baker County. Expansion into nearby regions is under review as resources and personnel increase.

What can you do?

The Maple County Paranormal Society relies heavily on public reports, observations, and shared information. Even seemingly minor or uncertain experiences may be valuable in identifying patterns or confirming active cases.

We are available for contact at all times. If you believe you have witnessed something unusual, or if you possess information related to an ongoing MCPS case, you are encouraged to report it through official channels.

Submitted information is reviewed by staff and may be incorporated into active investigations, archived for reference, or used in public awareness reporting when appropriate.

Volunteers who assist in identifying, documenting, or resolving active cases may be eligible for compensation depending on case classification and client authorization.
