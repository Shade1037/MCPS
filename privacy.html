Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18th, 2019

The Maple County Paranormal Society ("MCPS", "we", "our", or "the Society") is committed to protecting the privacy of all website users, members, and individuals who submit reports or communications through our official channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the MCPS website, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Basic contact information (such as email addresses submitted through contact forms)
  • Login credentials for member-access areas (where applicable)
  • Technical data including IP address, browser type, and access timestamps
  • Submitted reports, sightings, and case-related communications
  • Cookies and local session identifiers used to maintain website functionality

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used for the following purposes:

  • Responding to public inquiries and sighting reports
  • Maintaining member accounts and restricted access areas
  • Documenting and verifying paranormal case submissions
  • Improving website stability, security, and usability
  • Protecting ongoing investigations and field personnel safety

3. Data Sharing

The MCPS does not sell or distribute personal information to third-party advertisers.

Information may be shared internally within authorized Society members on a need-to-know basis.

The MCPS does not knowingly share case materials, reports, or user data with Z-Core Research, HazTek Industries, the HazTek Military Operations Force, or any affiliated corporate or governmental entities engaged in investigative suppression or interference activities.

4. Member Access & Restricted Data

Certain case files and investigation archives are restricted to approved MCPS members. Access to these materials is logged and monitored for security purposes.

Accounts found to be impersonating authorized users, or classified as Impersonators, may have access revoked without notice.

Individuals identified as Impersonators or humans exhibiting infection markers consistent with DK-HZMA-18 may be restricted from accessing all or part of the website, including public-facing content where necessary.

5. Cookies & Tracking

The MCPS website uses basic cookies and session storage to maintain login sessions, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure proper site functionality. No behavioral advertising tracking is used.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. However, no system connected to the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for operational, investigative, or safety purposes. Certain records may be archived indefinitely for historical case reference.

8. Children’s Privacy

The MCPS website is intended for general informational use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13 without appropriate consent where required.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as operational procedures evolve. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of any revised terms.
